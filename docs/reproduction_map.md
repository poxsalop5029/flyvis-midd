The exact source list for each figure is in [figure_inputs.json](../src/flyvis_midd/figure_inputs.json). Inputs shared by main and supplementary figures have one canonical location. Model/seed results, fixed acquisition contracts and model/stimulus identities are stored beside the corresponding scientific tables. [PROVENANCE.md](../data/precomputed/PROVENANCE.md) describes hashes and source limits.

Main Figure3/S7 share scalar fit/CV/comparator logic. Fig4/S5 share phase/geometry evidence; Fig5/S8 share learning and physical controls; Fig6/S9 share intervention metrics; S10 uses crossed-pair results and individual physical direction checks. Confidence intervals preserve their original cohort, fixed design, RNG specification and stored statistical definitions.

Fig1B replays archived raster snapshots because complete representative raw vectors are unavailable. Centered eight-field and native BoxEye contracts remain distinct. Official50 fixed diagnostic EPE is not held out. Raw upstream networks/Sintel and the custom checkpoint archive are separate acquisition dependencies; they are not needed for saved-input figure replay.

After generating figures when needed, build the manuscript with `python scripts/build_manuscript_pdf.py`. This assembles existing Figure PDFs, legends and Tables S1–S5B; Tables S5A–S5B and their supporting saved analyses are in `data/precomputed/supplementary/biological_constraints/`. Lightweight checks use `pytest` and `scripts/check_figure_integration.py`.

Figure notebooks replay saved inputs; acquisition routes that require external FlyVis assets are listed separately and are not required for figure replay. The manuscript PDF is assembled from the saved manuscript, figure PDFs, legends, and supplementary tables with `python scripts/build_manuscript_pdf.py`.
